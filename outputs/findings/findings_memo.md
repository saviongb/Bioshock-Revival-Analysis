Findings Memo: BioShock Trilogy Analysis

August 14th, 2026



Data: 85,395 reviews pulled from steams public review API (26,155 from BioShock 1, 12,008 from BioShock 2, 47,232 from Infinite). All English reviews from July 26th.



Method: keyword dictionaries created for nine themes - narrative, setting, atmosphere, characters, combat, ideology, visual/audio, technical performance, pacing. Analysis measured how often each theme appears per review, split by positive/negative verdict, review era, and hours played. Validated against 72 reviews checked manually for theme detection accuracy.



What do BioShock reviews talk about?

Claim — one sentence, no numbers. What the finding says.

Narrative is the franchises most consistent theme, revealing what players discuss when criticizing the game both positively and negatively. and the only theme mentioned in at least 20% of reviews for all three BioShock games. Of the verdict split for BioShock 1 and 2, findings are similar B1: 27.6% of positive reviews mention narrative compared to 10.6% negative. B2: 28.3% of positive reviews mention narrative compared to 11.9% negative. For Bioshock Infinite theme numbers are more split with 40.6% of positive reviews mentioning narrative and 38.4% of negative.
